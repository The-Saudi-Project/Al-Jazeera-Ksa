About Us من نحن

About Al Jazeera Service Contracting Co.

Al Jazeera Service Contracting Co. is a Saudi-based contracting and industrial support company established in 2010. Headquartered in Dammam, we deliver integrated contracting solutions across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Our scope covers human resource support, civil and industrial construction, mechanical works, equipment rental, maintenance services and specialised industrial support — engaged individually or combined into a single accountable contract.

With extensive experience across industrial, commercial, infrastructure and petrochemical sectors, we are committed to dependable solutions built on quality, safety, professionalism and long-term client partnerships.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To become the preferred contracting and industrial support partner across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia by consistently delivering dependable services with professionalism, integrity and excellence.

Our Mission

To provide reliable contracting, human resource services, construction, equipment rental and industrial support solutions that contribute to the success of our clients while supporting Saudi Arabia's continued development.

Instrumentation & Process Control Works

Turnkey instrumentation solutions, precision calibration, control valve servicing, impulse tubing and DCS/PLC loop testing engineered to the highest Saudi Aramco, SABIC and international industrial standards.

Discipline Capability

Precision Engineering for Critical Industrial Processes

In heavy petrochemical, refining, power and process plants, the reliability of instrumentation and safety systems is paramount. Al Jazeera Service Contracting Co. delivers integrated Electrical & Instrumentation (E&I) packages that minimise plant downtime and ensure complete measurement integrity.

From grassroots construction installations to complex plant turnaround revamps, our teams operate in full accordance with hazardous area standards (ATEX/IECEx/NEC), Saudi Aramco Engineering Standards (SAES), and stringent client QA/QC specifications.

Scope of Work

Comprehensive Instrumentation Scope

Delivering every phase of the instrument lifecycle — from procurement support and fabrication to bench calibration, field installation, and loop commissioning.

Field Transmitters & Sensors

Installation, bracket mounting, cabling and termination for pressure (PT), differential pressure (DPT), temperature (RTD/TC), Coriolis and vortex flowmeters, and radar/ultrasonic level transmitters.

Control Valves & Actuators

Pneumatic and motorized control valve overhaul, seat leakage testing, gland packing replacement, actuator diaphragm renewal, and HART/Fieldbus smart positioner calibration.

Instrument Tubing & Manifolds

Precision bending and routing of SS 316 / 316L, Monel and alloy tubing with Swagelok and Parker twin-ferrule fittings, 2/3/5-valve manifolds, and high-pressure decay integrity testing.

DCS, PLC & SCADA Loops

Complete loop checking from primary field sensing elements through marshalling cabinets to DCS/PLC I/O cards, verifying 4-20mA signals, Foundation Fieldbus, Profibus, and alarm interlocks.

Junction Boxes & Marshalling

Erection and internal wiring of flameproof (Ex-d) and increased safety (Ex-e) junction boxes, local control panels (LCP), intrinsic safety (IS) zener barriers, and signal isolators.

Fire & Gas (F&G) Systems

Deployment of optical UV/IR flame detectors, toxic H2S/CO gas monitors, LEL hydrocarbon detectors, manual call points, and Emergency Shutdown (ESD) interlocking devices.

Quality & Standards

Traceable Calibration & Rigorous Compliance

Every calibration and test performed by Al Jazeera utilizes certified, master reference standards traceable to international bodies (NIST / SASO).

  • Certified Master Standards: Fluke multi-function documenting process calibrators, Druck pressure calibrators, and dry-block temperature baths with active calibration certificates.
  • Hazardous Area Expertise: Certified technicians trained for Zone 0, Zone 1, and Zone 2 (Class 1 Div 1/2) explosive gas atmospheres.
  • Turnaround Readiness: Dedicated fly-in E&I teams equipped with mobile calibration trailers and workshop tooling for tight plant shutdown schedules.
  • Complete Deliverables: As-built loop drawings, calibration datasheets, punch-list closeout, and mechanical completion packages (MCP).

Capability Overview

Full spectrum contracting and industrial support

Al Jazeera Service Contracting Co. provides a comprehensive range of contracting, construction, maintenance, industrial support and IT services. Our integrated approach allows clients to engage a single contractor for multiple disciplines, streamlining project delivery and ensuring consistent quality across every workfront.

Service 06

Instrumentation Works

Precision process instrumentation, calibration, control valve servicing, loop checking and automated systems installation for industrial, oil & gas and petrochemical facilities across Saudi Arabia.

Key Features

  • Field transmitter installation, mounting & calibration (pressure, flow, level, temperature)
  • Control valve overhaul, stroke checking, actuator setup & smart positioner calibration
  • SS 316 / 316L instrument tubing, impulse line fabrication and pressure decay testing
  • Marshalling cabinets, local control panels (LCP) and junction box wiring
  • Distributed Control Systems (DCS), PLC & SCADA loop checking & point verification
  • Fire & Gas (F&G) detection systems, flame sensors and ESD interlock testing
  • Hazardous area certified installations (ATEX, IECEx, Class 1 Div 1/2)

How We Work

Our service delivery process

A structured approach from initial enquiry to project handover ensures consistency, transparency and accountability at every stage.

  1. Step 01

    Enquiry & Scope Review

    Client requirements are received and reviewed by our technical and commercial teams.

  2. Step 02

    Site Survey & Assessment

    Where required, a site visit is conducted to assess conditions and refine the scope of work.

  3. Step 03

    Proposal & Pricing

    A detailed technical and commercial proposal is submitted with clear pricing and delivery commitments.

  4. Step 04

    Mobilisation & Execution

    Resources are mobilised and the work is executed under professional supervision with regular progress reporting.

  5. Step 05

    Handover & Close out

    Completed work is inspected, documented and handed over with all required certifications and as built records.

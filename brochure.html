Service Lines 05 & 06

Electrical Works & Process Instrumentation

05. Electrical Engineering

Turnkey medium-voltage (MV) and low-voltage (LV) electrical distribution systems, substations, and plant electrification.

  • Substation equipment positioning & transformer cabling
  • Cable tray containment fabrication & conduit routing
  • Motor Control Centers (MCC), switchboards & VFD panels
  • Plant earthing (grounding) grids & lightning protection
  • Explosion-proof hazardous area lighting systems

  • Pressure, temperature, level & flow transmitter setup
  • Stainless steel impulse tubing & manifold hookups
  • PLC, SCADA, and DCS marshalling cabinet integration
  • Emergency Shutdown (ESD) & safety interlock testing
  • Cold & hot loop checking with certified calibrators

Calibration Accuracy: NIST traceable calibration standards and certified test logs.

HSEQ Commitment

Health, Safety, Environment & Quality

At Al Jazeera Service Contracting Co., the safety of our personnel and client worksites takes unconditional precedence over all operational demands.

Our Zero-Harm Safety Policy

We operate under a clear mandate: every worker returns home safely every single day. Our safety protocols match the demanding standards of Saudi Aramco and SABIC.

  • Mandatory daily morning Toolbox Talks (TBT)
  • Formal Task Risk Assessment (TRA) before work begins
  • Strict Permit-To-Work (PTW) protocols & audits
  • Mandatory certified PPE on all workfronts
  • Continuous safety refresher training for crews
Quality Assurance & Control

Certified under ISO 9001:2015, our comprehensive Project Quality Plans (PQP) enforce inspection benchmarks at every project milestone.

  • Material receiving inspection & Mill Test Reports
  • Stage-wise Inspection and Test Plans (ITP)
  • Third-party Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)
  • Certified hydro-testing and megger dielectric logs
  • Comprehensive client-approved handover dossiers

About Us من نحن

About Al Jazeera Service Contracting Co.

Al Jazeera Service Contracting Co. is a Saudi-based contracting and industrial support company established in 2010. Headquartered in Dammam, we deliver integrated contracting solutions across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Our scope covers human resource support, civil and industrial construction, mechanical, electrical and instrumentation works, maintenance services and equipment rental — engaged individually or combined into a single accountable contract.

With extensive experience across industrial, commercial, infrastructure and petrochemical sectors, we are committed to dependable solutions built on quality, safety, professionalism and long-term client partnerships.

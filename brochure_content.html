About The Company & Executive Statement

Corporate Narrative:
Al Jazeera Service Contracting Co. is a Saudi-based contracting and industrial support company established in 2010. Headquartered in Dammam, we deliver integrated contracting solutions across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Our scope covers human resource support, civil and industrial construction, mechanical, electrical and instrumentation works, maintenance services, and equipment rental — engaged individually or combined into a single accountable contract. With extensive experience across industrial, commercial, infrastructure, and petrochemical sectors, we are committed to dependable solutions built on quality, safety, professionalism, and long-term client partnerships.

Executive Statement:
"Over the past 15 years, Al Jazeera Service Contracting Co. has grown by maintaining a simple operating philosophy: single accountability, transparent client communication, and zero compromise on safety and engineering quality. As the Kingdom accelerates under Saudi Vision 2030, our team is equipped to support industrial operators and main contractors with dependable, compliant, and rapidly mobilized solutions."

Section 10

Health, Safety, Environment (HSE) & Quality Assurance

Zero-Harm Health, Safety & Environment Policy:
At Al Jazeera Service Contracting Co., the safety and physical well-being of our employees, client staff, and surrounding communities take absolute priority over all operational and schedule demands. Our target on every workfront is Zero Incidents, Zero Injuries, and Zero Environmental Harm.

HSE Operational Framework:

  • Task Risk Assessments (TRA): Comprehensive hazard identification conducted prior to commencing any work scope.
  • Daily Morning Toolbox Talks (TBT): Mandatory daily safety meetings conducted in multiple languages covering specific hazards, heat stress, working at heights, and emergency protocols.
  • Permit-to-Work (PTW) Compliance: Strict adherence to Hot Work, Cold Work, Confined Space Entry, and Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures.
  • Certified PPE: Mandatory provision and strict enforcement of certified protective personal equipment across all work sites.
  • Environmental Protection: Chemical containment trays, regulated hazardous waste disposal, spill response kits, and noise mitigation.

Quality Assurance & Quality Control (QA/QC):

  • Project Quality Plans (PQP) tailored to client and Aramco standards under ISO 9001:2015 certification
  • Material Receiving Inspections (MRI) verifying mill test certificates, heat numbers, and compliance logs
  • Stage-wise Inspection & Test Plans (ITP) verified by dedicated site QA/QC inspectors
  • Third-party Non-Destructive Testing (NDT): Radiography (RT), Ultrasonic (UT), Magnetic Particle (MPI), and Dye Penetrant (DPT)
  • Complete client-approved handover dossier including as-built drawings, warranties, test certificates, and operation manuals

About Us

Fifteen years building Saudi Arabia's industrial capability

Al Jazeera Service Contracting Co. has grown from a regional contractor into an integrated industrial support partner serving clients across the Kingdom.

Company Profile

Integrated contracting, delivered to industrial standards

Al Jazeera Service Contracting Co. is a Saudi-based contracting and industrial support company established in 2010. Headquartered in Dammam, the company delivers integrated contracting solutions including human resource support, civil and industrial construction, mechanical works, equipment rental, maintenance services and specialised industrial support across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

With extensive experience serving industrial, commercial, infrastructure and petrochemical sectors, Al Jazeera Service Contracting Co. is committed to delivering dependable solutions that emphasise quality, safety, professionalism and long-term client partnerships.

Our Journey

Steady growth, one contract at a time

Al Jazeera Service Contracting Co. expanded by adding capability where clients asked for it — first human resource support, then construction, then the plant and maintenance services that hold a scope together.

  1. 2010

    Company Established

    Founded in Dammam to provide contracting and human resource support services to Eastern Province industrial clients.

  2. 2013

    Expansion Across Eastern Province

    Extended capability into structural, concrete and site development works across the industrial corridor.

  3. 2016

    Industrial Services Growth

    Added mechanical works, equipment fleet and maintenance divisions to deliver end-to-end project solutions.

  4. 2019

    Diversified Service Lines

    Established dedicated teams for electrical, instrumentation and utility systems support.

  5. Today

    Kingdom-wide Operations

    Serving clients from Dammam and Jubail through Riyadh, Jeddah, Yanbu and Jizan with integrated industrial support.
